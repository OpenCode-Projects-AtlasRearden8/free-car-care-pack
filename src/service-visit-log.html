Service-Visit Receipt Log

Keep every oil change and repair on one tidy page — your car's medical record.

What this is

A running log of every visit: what was done, what it cost, and where the receipt lives. When you sell the car (or argue with a warranty claim), this page is gold. A complete service history can also raise resale value.

How to use it

  1. Print a few copies or print once and copy it.
  2. After each visit (even just an oil change), fill in one row.
  3. Tape or clip the paper receipt to the back of the sheet, or write the folder/app name where its photo is stored.
  4. Start a new sheet when one fills up — label them “Log 1 of _”.
Reminder: some services come with a warranty on the work itself (parts or labor). Write any warranty length in the “Notes” column so you can claim it if it fails early.
Printing tips: use card stock so the page survives glovebox life, or keep the whole stack in a 1/2-inch binder in the trunk.
