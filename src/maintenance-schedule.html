Maintenance Schedule — Fill-in by Model

A one-page service calendar you fill in for YOUR car, then keep in the glovebox.

What this is

Every car is a little different. This sheet has no pre-filled intervals — instead, you look up the real numbers for your model and write them in. That way it matches your actual vehicle, not a generic guess.

How to use it (10 minutes, one time)

  1. Write your car's year, make, model, and engine at the top.
  2. Find your owner's manual (paper copy, or free PDF from the maker's website — search “yourmake owner's manual PDF”).
  3. Look up the “recommended maintenance schedule” or “service intervals” chapter and copy the intervals onto this sheet.
  4. Pick a good oil-change shop and note it at the bottom.
  5. Paper-clip this to the glovebox and update the odometer box at each service visit.
Reminder: intervals vary a lot between models and even between trim levels (some engines are 10,000 miles, some are 5,000). Always copy the numbers from your manual — this printable just gives them a home.
Printing tips: print at 100% scale on regular paper. A damaged spot in the glovebox is fine — just print a new one. You can also fill it in on a tablet if your PDF app lets you type.
