Used-Car Test-Drive & Inspection Checklist

Ten calm minutes of looking and listening that can save you thousands.

What this is

A print-and-fill checklist for shopping used cars, private sale or dealer lot. It won't replace a mechanic's inspection — it's the first, free, 20-minute filter that most people skip.

How to use it

  1. Print a copy per car you're seriously considering. Fill the top with the year/make/model/price.
  2. Page 2: the look-over, on the lot, before you even drive.
  3. Page 3: the test-drive with a co-pilot when possible (four ears beat two).
  4. Score honestly. Two or more red circles = walk away or price your way in.
Reminder: your strongest single move: pay an independent mechanic $100–150 for a pre-purchase inspection on any car you'd actually buy. No seller who's honest will refuse. Also run the VIN through a history-report service (CARFAX or the NMVTIS alternatives) yourself.
Printing tips: bring a pen on a string or one that clips to the clipboard. Cold engines hide problems — ask to see it cold-start if the seller lets you arrange things that way.

Used-Car Look-Over — Before the Drive

Car: ____________________ Seller: ____________________ Asking: $__________
  • Odometer recorded: ______ (and photo taken)
  • Title available, in seller's name, matches who you're talking to — title lien line clear (“clean title”)
  • Walk around level ground: car sits even (a corner riding low hints suspension or a sway bar)
  • Tires: even wear side to side; same brand pair front/rear; no steel belts showing
  • Panel gaps: consistent lines hood/doors/fenders (wavy gaps can hint a crash repair)
  • Paint: same tone everywhere in daylight (masking-tape “color shift” is a respray clue)
  • Rust: look at wheel wells, door bottoms, and under the tailgate/rockers
  • Lights: every bulb outside works, lenses not fogged-to-mud
  • Under the car where parked: any drips, spots, or puddle stains
  • Engine bay (engine off & cool): no cracked belts, hoses feel pliant not crunchy, oil cap not milky
  • Open all four doors, hood, trunk: all open and latch without fighting
  • Seats/belts/airbag light: belts pull and retract; airbag light ON = hard pass
  • Check ALL warning lights light up at key-on, then go off (a dead cluster bulb this close to sale is rarely innocent)
  • Trunk: spare tire present + inflated, jack/tools present
  • Ask for records: any service history? Manual in glovebox?
  • Ask your age question: how many owners, any crashes, any flood claim? (Write answers)

Verify with your manual: if the sale happens, the same day, pull the maker's official owner's-manual PDF (free) and note this model's known service intervals + PSI — you'll want page 1 of the maintenance schedule filled in from it.

Used-Car Test-Drive

Engine cold at start if possible. Plan ~15 minutes: slow streets, one highway ramp, one parking lot.
Start-up (before you even drive)
  • Cold start: fires quickly, settles to a calm idle — no shaking, revving, or dieseling
  • Interior: no fuel, rotten-egg, or burning smells at idle
  • Under the hood after start: no hissing or ticking that doesn't fade
  • Rear view camera + screens: work (they're expensive modules)
While driving
  • Accelerates smoothly, no hesitation or shudder
  • Transmission shifts without clunks, slips, or high-rev “lag”
  • Brakes: firm pedal, straight stop, no grind or squeal
  • Steering: wheel straight on level road; car doesn't wander
  • One hard-ish stop: no pulling sideways, ABS doesn't trigger on dry road
  • Full-lock turn both ways (parking lot): no clicking or binding up front (FWD especially)
  • Bumps and seams: one thud per bump — not clunks, rattles, or double-hits
  • At highway speed: no droning (bearings), no vibration in wheel or seat
  • Turn off radio for part of the drive and just listen
  • HVAC runs hot and cold, fan makes no leaf-blower noises
  • After the drive, engine off: fluid smells, steam, or fresh drip under the car?
