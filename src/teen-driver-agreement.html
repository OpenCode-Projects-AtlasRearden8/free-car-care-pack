Teen-Driver Family Agreement Card

A signed, expected-of-everyone agreement — kindness with a signature, not rules delivered at the door.

What this is

A two-page printable your family fills in together, signs together, and keeps in the sun visor. It's not a punishment tool: it's a shared agreement where everyone — parent and teen — has written promises. That's what makes it stick.

How to use it

  1. Set aside 30 relaxed minutes together (not in the driveway before a first solo drive).
  2. Fill in both “The driver promises” and “The grown-up promises” — the reciprocity is the whole trick. Teens agree to rules when adults agree to trust and to be reachable, no questions asked, 2 a.m. included.
  3. Both of you sign. Both keep a copy: visor + kitchen corkboard.
  4. Review it at 3 months, and renegotiate anything that isn't working — for either side.
Reminder: your state has graduated driver license (GDL) laws — passenger limits, night curfews, phone rules. Check your state's DMV page and copy those into this card as the baseline; then the card adds your family's layer on top.
Printing tips: card stock for the copy that lives in the visor. Keep the tone warm when filling it in — this is a safety pact, not a contract for court. If a promise can't be kept on a bad day, it's the wrong promise; shrug and rewrite it.
