Glovebox Info Card

The card that answers everything when something goes wrong — or when someone else is driving your car.

What this is

A two-sided cheat sheet for the glovebox: key car numbers, who to call, what to do in a breakdown, and what to say after a fender-bender. In a stressful moment, having it written down beats digging through your phone.

How to use it

  1. Fill in your car, insurance, and emergency contacts. Write phone numbers out fully — a locked phone doesn't help a borrowed car.
  2. Fold in half, so the “In an emergency” side faces out.
  3. Leave it in the glovebox, on top of the manual.
Reminder: the phone number for roadside assistance or insurance claims is usually printed on your insurance card and in your owner's manual's first pages — copy it exactly from those sources.
Printing tips: print on card stock if you have it. Privacy-minded? Use first names and a P.O. box or shop address — the car doesn't need your home address riding around in it.
